# Medial Axis Transform

*1 Nov 2024 – 15 Dec 2024*

## Project Overview

The Medial Axis Transform project is a final project for COMP 558 (Computer Vision) at McGill University. The main goal is to replicate and build upon the findings of the paper "Shape-Based Measures Improve Scene Categorization" by exploring additional geometric properties in images.

## Objectives

- **Replication**: Recreate the experiments from the referenced paper to establish a baseline.
- **Innovation**: Propose a new geometric property, Convexity, to complement traditional Gestalt cues.
- **Evaluation**: Investigate the impact of these geometry cues on enhancing CNN model performance through rigorous experiments.

## Technical Approach

- **Data Analysis**: Employ image processing techniques to extract and analyze relevant geometric features.
- **Model Training**: Integrate the new geometric property into CNN models and evaluate performance gains.
- **Comparative Study**: Compare results with baseline approaches to ascertain the effectiveness of the proposed cue.

## Outcomes

This project aims to bridge theoretical insights and practical implementation, ultimately contributing to the field of computer vision by offering improved methods for scene categorization.

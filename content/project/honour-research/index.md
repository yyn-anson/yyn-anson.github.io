# Honour Research Project

*30 Nov 2024 – Current*

## Overview

This project aims to detect and quantify misinformation and bias on social media by employing advanced social network analysis techniques. It integrates article embedding methods, prompt engineering for multi-agent simulations, and comprehensive network analysis to assess the effects of bias on user engagement.

## Methodology

- **Article Embedding**: Quantifies the political leaning of news articles using state-of-the-art embedding techniques.
- **Prompt Engineering**: Implements advanced simulation strategies for effective bias rating through multi-agent systems.
- **Social Network Analysis**: Evaluates the impact of misinformation on social media dynamics via thorough network analysis.

## Supervision

Mentored by Professor Benjamin C. M. Fung and Elena Obukhova.

## Impact

The outcomes of this research are expected to provide crucial insights for combating the spread of biased content and securing more reliable information channels online.
